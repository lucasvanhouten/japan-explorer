# The catalogue — Snow country & Tōhoku

This is the whole catalogue for this region, not the kit's shortlist: it includes the inns the shortlist leaves out on price, on score or by name, which is what makes it the place to look when the shortlist has no answer for a stop. The shortlist for the same ground is `stays/sendai.md`. Column meanings and the tier bands are at the top of `catalog/catalog.md`; the live site is https://ryokancatalog.com, and any row's full record is `https://ryokancatalog.com/api/inn/<slug>.json`.*
