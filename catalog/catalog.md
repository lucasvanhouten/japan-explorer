# Ryokan Finder — full catalog index

aspects (Food · Bath · Service · Room · Place) are 1–5. Tiers: **S** build the trip around it · **A** anchor a leg · **B** right inn, right place · **C** situational · **D** the evidence warns.
An inn nobody has written up first-hand is *unscored* — its Ikyu (Japanese booking site)
crowd rating out of 5 is given instead.
